# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this roadmap accurate.

- **Broken or outdated link:** open an issue with the file and the link, or send a pull request with a replacement from an official source.
- **New resource:** prefer official docs, papers and engineering blogs over paid courses or listicles. Say which week and lesson it supports.
- **A fact or number:** include the source link and its date. Market figures must say where they come from.
- **Tone:** plain English, short sentences, no hype.

By contributing you agree your contribution is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
